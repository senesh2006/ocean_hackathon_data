Regulations
1. These regulations may be cited as the Bolgoda Reservoir. (Fishing Operations) Regulations, 2007.
2. These regulations shall apply to every licence holder who engages in fishing operation in the Bolgoda Reseryoir.
3. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained in the Fishing Operations Regulations of 1996, no licence
holder 'shall engage in any fishing operations in the Bolgoda Reservoir, unless the licence issued to such licence holder bears
and endorsemenit to the effect that it is “valid for Bolgoda Reservoir".
4. No person shall for the purpose of taking fish in the Bolgoda Reservoir -
. use any net other than a gill net, a cast net or a drift net ;
(q)
erect any structure other than a Jakotuwa ; or
use any net of mesh size less than four inches (101.6 mm.).
5: The set of nets that could be in a boat (oru) shall be as follows -
(a)
 maximum number of net pieces in a set of nets, shall be limited to four (4) ; and
: maximum number of mesh in.the length of a net shall be one hundred and fifty (150)meshes and the mesh in
the breadth of the net-shall'be forty (40) meshes.:
