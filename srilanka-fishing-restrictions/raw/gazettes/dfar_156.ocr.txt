Regulations
1. These regualtions may be cited as the Fishery Management (Batticaloa Lagoon) Regulations of 2001.
specified in the Schedule hereto, unless the license issued to such license holder bears and endorsement to the effect i.e. "valid
for Batticaloa Lagoon Management area".
3. For the purpose of these regulations, the Batticaloa Lagoon Management Area (in these regulations referred to as
the "Area') is divided into three sectors in the manner as specified in the Schedule.
4. Karaivalai or drag net (madel) fishing operations are prohibited in the Area while cast nets or veechuvalai and
fishing lines may be used in any part of the said Area.
5. No license holder shall use --
(a)
any drift net or adasivalai with a mesh less than 7.62 cm (3 inches) in Sector 2; and
(b)
any net other than a cast net or veechuvalai and fishing lines in Sector 1 and 3,
as specified in the schedule hereto.
6. No license holder shall use --
(a)
any incandescent lamp or other artificial light other than a hurricane lantern or chulu light for the purpose
of taking fish in the lagoon;
(b)
any net other than a cast net or veechuvalai at the mouth of the lagoon or within a radius of 183.0 Meters
(200 yards) from the mouth; or
(c)
a mechanized fishing boat in the lagoon for purpose of taking fish.
7. No license holder shall erect kottus or enclosures for fishing in any 'part of the Area, provided that a committee
consisting of the District Secretary and Assistant Director of Fisheries of the district, may, at their discretion, permit the erection
of kottus or enclosures for prawn fishing, specifying the size of such kottus or enclosures and the Area where they may be
erected.
8. In the event where any fisherman who is associated with the use of a fishing net do not remain at the site of the
fishing operation the license holders name and license number, shall be displayed in letters not less than 5.2 cm (2 inches) in
height and 3.9 cm (one and half inches) in width on floats (attached to the net) is spaced at intervals of 50 meters (55 yards)
in an easily recognizable manner.

9. Fishing operations of kottus or enclosures shall display their names and license number, in letters not less than
5.2 cm (2 inches) in height and 3.9 cm (one and half inches) in width, painted on a board and fixed in a manner that will provide
for easy recognition.
10. In these regualtions --
"Adasivalai or drift net"' means a net set at the surface layers of water,
" Karaivalai or drag net (madel)" means a type of traditional drag net consisting of two wing nets to
which the bag net or the belly is atached at the end, and.having a codend or "Madiya" to which the fish
are finally driven into and two coirropes tied to the other end of the two wing nets extending up to the
sea shore, which are set in the coastal waters with the help of manually operated boats partially
surrounding the fish and thereafter gradually dragged or pulled ashore by two groups of people;
" Kattus or enclosures" means a set net consisting of a conical or cylindrical trap with or without a
frame and with a device which draws the fish into the net;
" Lincense holder" means a person who is in possession of a valid license issued under the Fishing
Operations Regulations, 1996; and
c Veechuvalai or cast net" means any cone shapped net having a wide mouth opening of about 4.572
to 6.096 meters (15 to 20 feet) radius, operated in shallow waters by an individual by throwing such
net in to the water.
