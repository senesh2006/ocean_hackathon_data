CHAPTER 567
                              FAUNA AND FLORA PROTECTION
       AN ORDINANCE TO PROVIDE FOR THE PROTECTION AND
       CONSERVATION OF THE FAUNA AND FLORA OF SRI LANKA
       AND THEIR HABITATS ; FOR THE PREVENTION OF
       COMMERCIAL AND OTHER MISUSE OF SUCH FAUNA AND
       FLORA AND THEIR HABITATS, FOR THE CONSERVATION OF
       THE BIODIVERSITY OF SRI LANKA ; AND TO PROVIDE FOR
       MATTERS CONNECTED THEREWITH OR INCIDENTAL
       THERETO.
