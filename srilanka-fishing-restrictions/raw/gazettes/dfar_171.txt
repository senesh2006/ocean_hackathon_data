Regulations No. 1 of 2023.

         2.	Every permit to import any species of coral which are listed in the Convention on International Trade in
Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora (CITES) shall be obtained from Department of Wild Life conservation and in
respect of other species a license shall be issued by the Department of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources under section 39 of
the Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Act as Specified in Schedule 1I hereto.

         3.        A person shall not import or export any coral species cultured on artficial substrates unless such coral
species is cultured as per the culture of Coral species on artificial substrates for export regulations No. 1 of 2017 published
in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2032/5 of August 14,2017.

       4.      The substrates used for the culture of coral species for export shall be approved by the National Aquaculture
Development Authority established under the National Aquaculture Development Authority of Sri Lanka Act, No. 53 of
1998.

         5.        An application for a licence to export cultured coral species on artificial substrates shall be made
substantially in the Form II specified in Schedule I hereto.

         6.        The Director General shall, upon receipt of such application specified in regulation 5, issue a licence
substantially in the Form IV specified in Schedule II hereto subject to the terms and conditions specified in the licence with
the approval of the National Aquaculture Development Authority.

        7.        Such licence shall be valid for a period of six months from the date of issue unless cancelled earlier for
contravention of the terms and conditions of the licence or any provision of these regulations.

         8.        An application for a licence to import cultured coral species on artificial substrates shall be made
substantially in the Form I specified in Schedule I hereto.

         9.        The Director General shall, upon receipt of such application specified in regulation 8, issue a licence
substantially in the Form III specified in Schedule II hereto subject to the terms and conditions specified in the licence, with
the approval of the National Aquaculture Development Authority.

        10.       Such licence shall be valid for a period of six months from the date of issue unless cancelled earlier for
contravention of the terms and conditions of the licence or any provision of these regulations.

           11.     In these regulations –

         “Director-General” means the Director-General of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources appointed under Section 2 of
the Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Act, No. 2 of 1996.
