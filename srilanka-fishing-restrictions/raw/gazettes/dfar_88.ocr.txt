Regulations
1.These regulations may be cited as the Negombo Lagoon Fishery Management Area Regulations of 1998.
2. These regulations shall apply to fishing operations in the management area of the Negombo Lagoon morefully described in the Schedule
hereto, (Hereinafter referred to as the **Negombo Lagoon Management Area").
3.Notwithstanding anything contained in any other written law to the contrary,all licences for engaging in fishing operations issued under
provisions of Fishing Operations Regulations of 1996, published in the Gazette Extraordinary No. 948/25 of 7th November, 1996 shall not be valid
within the Negombo Lagoon Are , unless these licences are endorsed with the condition "Valid for Negombo Lagoon Management Area".
4.Fishing operations us g the following gears and methods shall be prohibited in the "Negombo Lagoon Management Area":
(a) Trawl Net fishing ope. itions;
(b) Push Net fishing operations;
(c) Moxi Net fishing operations;
(d).Gill Net or Trammel Net fishing operations on coral reefs or rocks:
(e) Digging out of polychaete worms.
5.Trammel nets shall be used in the Negombo Lagoon Fishery Management Area only during the hours from 6 p. m. to 6 a.m.
6.The total quantity of trammel (disco) nets during a fishing operation shall not exceed six hundred metres in top rope length.
7The possession of trammel nets whose combined top.rope length exceeds six hundred metres in a craft within the lagoon shall be taken as
evidence that the operator is in contravention of Regulation 6 of these Regulations.
8.All fishing operations shall be prohibited within one hundred and fifty metresfrom the shores of the Negombo Lagoon Management Area
(and the islands contained within) other than those using stake net (Kattu del), cast nets (visi del), crab traps (kakulu athangawa), small hand-held
shrimp nets (malissa athanguwa), fishing rods/handlines (bili piththa/ath yotha), manual fish trap and lantern (Karakgediya and Pandama)and eel
traps (irati/kemana).

SCHEDULE
(Regulation 2)
DEFINITION OF NEGOMBO LAGOON MANAGEMENTAREA
In these Regulations the Negombo Lagoon Management Area is defined on the seaward side by an imaginary straight line drawn between
Kutti Duwa and Duwa Point and within the lagoon to the high tide level, and including the mouths of streams and rivers flowing into the lagoon.
