Regulations
1. These regulations may be cited as the Regulation of the Landing of Fish (Species of Shark'and Skates)
Regulations, 2001.
2. A licence holder may land fish belonging to the species of Shark or Skate, so long as the fins of such species of.
fish are attached to such fish.
3. No licence holder shall land only the fins which have been removed from any fish belonging to the species of
Shark or Skate.
4. In these regulations-
" Licence holder " means. a person who is in possession of a valid licence issued under the Fishing Operations
Regulations of 1996 published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 948/25 of November 07, 1996.
