REGULATIONS
1. 
 These regulations may be cited as the Lobster Fisheries Management Regulations of 2000.
2.  No licence holder shall engage in catching spiny lobster within the sea area specified in Schedule I hereto, unless the licence
issued to such holder bears an endorsement to the effect:that it is:"valid for the taking of south coast'spiny lobster:"
3.
(1) No licence holder shall, notwithstanding anything to the contrary in the licence or any other regulation, engage in
- catching spiny lobster in the sea area specified in Schedule I hereto during the months of-February and September;
(2) Possession of spiny lobster, parts of spiny lobster or equipment for the capture of spiny lobster within a fishing boat
4.  No person shall purchase, sell, transport or be in possession of spiny lobster :-
(1) of the species Panulirus ornatus having acarapace length less than ten centimeters, or of tail length less than fourteen
centimeters (in the event the Carapace being not available for measurement);
(2) of species other than the species referred to in paragraph(1), having a carapace length less than six centimeters, or of
tail. length.less. than ten centimeters (in the event the carapace being not available for measurement);
(3), of whose measurement cannot be taken for the purpose of paragraphs (1) or (2); or
(4) in the soft - shell condition:
5.
(1) No'person shall -
external eggs; or
(b)  remove the eggs or any of the pleopods to which the eggs attach, from spiny lobster or slipper lobster with'external 
eggs.
(2) The possession of spiny lobster.or slipper lobster, or parts thereof, from which the external eggs or any of the pleopods to
. which the eggs attach have been removed, will be prima facie evidence of such person having removed the eggs.
6.No person, other than a Permit holder, shall be in possession, or exhibit for sale, sell or transport flesh of a spiny lobster or slipper
lobster except under the:authority of a permit issued for that purpose by the Director.

10. Spiny lobster and Prawn Regulations of 1973 are hereby resinded.
11. In these regulations. --
"Carapace"' means the outer cover of the cephalothorax or fused portion of the head and thorax (see figure 1).
"Carapace Length" means the length of the carapace measured from the mid point of the anterior border of the carapace between
the base of the two rostral horns, along the mid-line of the dorsal surface to the posterior. border of the carapace
(see figure 1).
"Directior" means the Directior of Fisheries and Aquatic'Resources appointed under Section 2 of the Fisheries and Aquatic
Resources Act, No. 2 of 1996.
°Licence Holder" means a person who is in possession ofa licence issued under Fishing Operations Regulations of 1996, for catching
spiny lobsrer or slipper lobster.
Slipper Lobster" means all species of the family Seyllaridae. Shown in figure 2 of these Regulations. 
"Soft Shell" means the new shell of the lobster after moulting before it has hardened.
 Panulirus homarus, Panulirus longipes. Panulirus versicolor, Panulirus ornatus, Panulirus penicillatus and Panulirus poliphagus
"Tail Length'" means the measurement along the mid-line of the dorsal surface ofthe tail, from the anterior edge ofthe first abdominal
segment to the posterior edge of the telson, taken with the tail straight and pressed flat, and the ventral surface downwards
(see figure 1).
"Telson" means the terminal abdominal joint of the tail (see figure 1).
