Regulations
1. These regulations may be cited as the Export and Import of Live Fish Regulations, 1998.
2. No person shall export any species of live fish set out in Part I of the Schedule hereto.
3. No person shall except under the authority of a permit issued to him by the Director of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources
(hereinafter referred to as the " Director ") in that behalf export any species oflive fish set out in Part II of the Schedule hereto.
4. No person shall import any species of live fish set out in Part Ill of the Schedule hereto.
5. Every application for a permit under Regulation 3 shall be made to the Director in the Form 'A' set out in Part IV of the Schedule
hereto and shall be accompanied with a fee of One Thousand Rupees.
6.Every permit issued upon and application made under regulation 5 shall be in the Form " B " set out in Part IV of the Schedule
hereto and shall unless revoked earlier, be valid for six months from the date of issue, and subject to the terms and conditions specified therein.
7.  Notwithstanding Regulation 2 and Regulation 3, any person may under the authority of a permit issued by the Director in that
behalf export any live fish imported.
8. Every application for a permit under Regulation 7 shall be made to the Director in the Form “ C "' set out in Part IV of the
Schedule hereto.
9.Every permit issued upon an application made under Regulation 8 shall be in the Form “" D " set out in part IV of the Schedule
hereto, and shall be subject to the terms and conditions specified therein.

14.  In these Regulations.-
“" Director " means the Director of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources appointed under Section 2 of the Fisheries and Aquatic
Resources Act, No. 2 of 1996.
" export " means to take out of the limits of Sri Lanka to sea or to a foreign country.
“" Fish " means any aquatic organism, whether piscine or not, and includes any shellfish, crustacean, coral, molluse, annelid,
holothurian, any other invertebrate, amphibian, aquatic mammal and its young fry, egg or spawn.
" import " means to bring into Sri Lanka, by sea or by air, from any place outside or beyond the limits of Sri Lanka.

Regulations
These regulations may be cited as the Fish Processing Establishments Regulations, 1998.
No person shall, except under the authority of a license issued by the Director of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (hereinafter
referred to as the “Director") in that behalf, operate a fish processing establishment.
Every application for a licence referred to in Regulation 2 shall be made to the Director, in Form "A" set out in the Schedule
3.
hereto, together with a fee of one thousand rupees and shall be accompanied by the certificate of registration issued to such
establishment by Sri Lanka Standards Institution established by the Act, No. 6 of 1984.
Every licence issued upon an application made under regulation 3 shall be in Form "B" set out in the Schedule hereto and shall,
unless revoked earlier be valid for one year from the date of issue, and shall be subject to the terms and conditions specified
therein.
5.
The Director may revoke a licence issued under regualtion 4, if he is satisfied --
(a) That the licensee has violated any of the terms and conditions of such licence ; or
(b) That the certificate of registration issued by the Sir Lanka Standards Institution to such licence has been cancelled.
6.
. Regulations published in the Gazette Extraordinary, No. 972/14 of April 25, 1997 ia hereby rescinded.
In these regulations-
"Fish Processing Establishment" means any premises where fish are prepared, processed, packaged, frozen or stored.
