                        FISHERIES AND AQUATIC RESOURCES ACT, No. 02 OF 1996

Regulations made by the Minister of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Development under section 61 read with Section 30
of the Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Act, No. 2 of 1996.

                                                                                  DR. RAJITHA SENARATNE,
                                                                Minister of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Development.

Ministry of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Development,
Colombo,
30th July, 2010.

                                                         Regulations

         1. These regulations may be cited as "the Fishing (Import and Export) Regulations 2010".

         2. No person shall take, collect, harvest remove fish, fish products or aquatic resources for import and Export
without obtaining a valid fishing Operation License issued by the Director-General for that purpose under subsection (1) of
section 6 of the Act, and any regulation made and published in Gazette Extraordinary, No.948/25 dated November 7, 1996.

         3. The Director-General may determine the number of fishing operation licenses to be issued in respect of any
specified area.
          4. No person shall engage in any dredging at the sea bed or undertake trawling operations within Sri Lanka Waters
in relation to any activities specified in this regulation for which a fishing operation license has been issued.

         5. In terms of section 34 of the Act, the Minister shall publish a Notice declaring a “closed season” or an “open
season” and the time of the day during which the activities referred to in relation to any specified fishing area, is prohibited.

         6. Form 1, Form 2, Form 3, Form 4, Form 5, Form 6, Form 7 and Form 8 specified in Schedule I to these regulation shall
be used by any person applying for any specified category of fishing license referred to in Schedule II under this regulation:-

            (a)   Application to possess, exhibit for sale, sell or transport any category of fish            Form 1
            (b)   License for possession, exhibiting for sale, selling or transporting                        Form 2
            (c)   Application for an Export Licence for the Export of any Category of Fish                    Form 3

                                                      SCHEDULE II

                                              BECHE-DE-MER FISHING LICENCE

          1. "Beche-de-mer" means Sea Cucumber species which belong to the class of Holothuroidea.

          2. Activities for which a license is required shall be as follows. Any incomplete Form shall be rejected:-

                 (1) Application for the export of boiled and dried (processed) Beche-de-mer.       Form 3
                 (2) License for the export of boiled and dried (processed) Beche-de-mer            Form 4

          3. Every license issued under Form 2 of Schedule I, shall be valid for a period of six months, commencing from 1st
             January and 1st July, if not cancelled earlier. A permit issued shall be subject to such terms and conditions as
             may be stipulated therein.

          4. A fee of two rupees (Rs. 2) shall be charged for a kilogram of Beche-de-mer. This sum shall be paid when
             applying for a license for the export of boiled and dried (processed) Beche-de-mer.
