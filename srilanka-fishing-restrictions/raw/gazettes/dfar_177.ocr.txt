Regulations
1. These regulations may be cited as the Parakrama Samudraya Fisheries Management Area Regulations of 2002.
2. These Regulations shall apply to the Fisheries Management Area of the Parakrama Samudraya described in the
Schedule hereto (hereinafter referred to as the "Fisheries Management Area ").
3. Notwithstanding anything contained in any other written law to the contrary, no license holder shall engage in any
fishing operations within the Fisheries Management Area unless the license issued to such license holder bears an endorsement
to the effect that it is " yalid for Parakrama Samudraya Management Area "
4. No person shall use any fishing gear other than the gill nets within the Fisheries Management Area, and the
maximum number of gill nets and the boats (Oru) that could be used, shall be determined by the Parakrama Samudraya Fisheries
Management Committee.
5. No person shall use any gill net of mesh size less than four inches (101.6 mm), and the set of will nets that could be
in a boat (Oru) shall be as foilows : -
(a) maximum number of net pieces in a set of gill nets, shall be limited to five (05) ; and
(b) maximum length of a net piece shall not exceed one thousand and five hundred (1,500) meshes.
