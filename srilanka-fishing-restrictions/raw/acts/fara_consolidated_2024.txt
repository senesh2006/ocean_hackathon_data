                     FISHERIES AND AQUATIC RESOURCE

 AS ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE MANAGEMENT. REGULATION, CONSERVATION AND
 DEVELOPMENT OF FISHERIES AND AQUATIC RESOURCES IN SRI LANKA;TO GIVE
  EFFECT TO SRI LANKA'S OBLIGATIONS UNDER CERTAIN INTERNATIONAL AND
  REGIONAL FISHERIES AGREEMENTS; TO REPEAL THE FISHERIES ORDINANCE
(CHAPTER 212), THE CHANK FISHERIES ACT (CHAPTER 213), THE PEARL FISHERIES
ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 214) AND THE WHALING ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 215) ; AND
 TO PROVIDE FOR MATTERS CONNECTED THERE WITH OR INCIDENTAL. THERETO.
