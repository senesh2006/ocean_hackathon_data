Regulations
1.Theseregulations may be cited as Fishing Operations Regulations of 1996 and shall come into operation on the
first day of December, 1996.
2.(a) No person shall catch, land, transport, sell, buy, receive or have in his possession any marine mammals or
turtles....
(b) The following fishing operations are hereby prohibited :-
(i)Push Net fishing operations;
(i)Harpooningformarinemammals;
(iid) Moxi Net fishing operations;
(iy) Gill Net or Trammel Net fishing operations on coral reefs or rocks.
3.No person shallengage in, or cause any other person to engage in, any fishing operation specified in PartI of the
Schedule hereto, in the sea, estuaries or coastal lagoons of Sri Lanka except under the authority of a licence issued under these
regulations and otherwise than in accordance with the terms and conditions attached to such licence..
4.Every application for a licence refered to in Regulation 3 shall be substantially in Form "A"in Part II of the
Schedulehereto,and shall be sentby registered post to the Licensing Officerofthe AdministrativeDistrict in which therelevant
fishing operation is to be carried out, and shall be accompanied by the relevant licence fee specified in Part Ill of the Schedule
hereto, Application shall be made by the owner of the boat (if such boat is used in the fishing operation) or by the owner of
the fishing equipment, as the case may be. Where a Licensing Officer has not been appointed for any Administrative District,
such application may be addressed to the Director ofFisheries and Aquatic Resources (hereinafterreferred to as "the Director").

in force for a period of one year from the date of the licence unless it is cancelled earlier under Section lo of the Fisheries and

applying forrenewal of such licence.

Regulations
1.These regulations may be cited as the InlandFisheries Management Regulations of 1996 and shall come into
operation on the first day of December,1996.
2.These regulations shall apply to and in relation to all parts of inland waters of Sri Lanka.
3.No person shall, except under the authority of a licence issued by the Director or by a licensing officer specially
authorized in writing by the Director in that behalf, use or operate any type of fishing gear other than a rod and line for the
purpose of taking fish in any part of the Inland Waters described in the Schedule hereto.
4.A licence shall not be issued under these regulations for the following types of fishing gear or boats:--
(i)Any net of mesh size 85 mm or below;
(i)Any surrounding or towing net (such as madel, purse seine, trawl net):
)Any trammel net;
(iv)Anynetmadeof monofilamentnetting material.
(v)Anymechanised boat.
5.Any person, being the owner of a boat orfishing gear equipment, who wishes to obtain a licence under regulation
3may make a written application subtantially in Form"A"set out in the Schedule hereto, to the Director or a licensing officer
specially authorized in writing in that behalf by the Director to receive such applications together with the licensing fee:
(a) of Rupees Fifty (Rs. 50.00) in the case of a set of gill nets;
(b) of Rupees Twenty-Five (Rs.25.00) in the case of other fishing gear.
